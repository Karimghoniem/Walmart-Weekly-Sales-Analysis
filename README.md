## 📌 Project: Weekly Sales Analysis of Walmart Stores

### 📝 Description:
This project focuses on analyzing the **weekly sales performance** of Walmart retail stores across the U.S.  
The objective is to uncover patterns and relationships between sales and external factors like:
- Holidays
- Fuel prices
- CPI (Consumer Price Index)
- Unemployment rates
- Temperature

These insights support data-driven decisions in **marketing, promotions, and supply chain planning**.

---

### 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used:
- **Power BI** – for interactive dashboards and data visualization
- **Excel / CSV** – for data preprocessing and import
- **DAX** – for calculated columns and KPIs in Power BI
- **Python** *(optional)* – for data cleaning or deeper statistical analysis

---

### 🔍 Types of Analysis:
- Weekly sales comparison across different stores
- Holiday impact on sales
- Time series analysis to detect trends
- Correlation analysis with external economic indicators
- Seasonality & cyclical pattern detection

---

### 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
- Total weekly sales across all stores
- Average weekly sales per store
- Top 5 performing stores based on sales
- Week-over-week sales change (%)
- Sales performance: holiday vs. non-holiday periods

---

### 📈 Visualizations:
- Line charts – sales trends over time
- Bar charts – store performance comparison
- Pie charts – sales distribution (holiday vs. non-holiday)
- Slicers – to filter by store, date range, or holiday flag
- Heatmaps – sales patterns vs. external indicators

---

### ✅ Key Outcomes:
- Identified high-performing and underperforming stores
- Found clear trends and peak sales periods
- Discovered correlations between economic conditions and sales
- Provided insights to improve store strategy, operations, and promotion targeting
